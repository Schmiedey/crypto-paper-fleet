Go-live gate

Rule, fixed on 2026-10-08 before seeing the results: after 90 days live, a strategy may trade small real money only if its live Sharpe is ≥ 0.5 and its worst drop is no deeper than 1.5× its backtest's. What the numbers assume

  • Crypto fills pay Kraken Pro's tier-3 taker fee, 0.38% per side (needs $10k+ monthly volume or $20k held). A small new account pays 0.60–0.80%, which wipes out most of these edges.
  • QQQ trades cost 5 bp per side; the intraday Nasdaq bot assumes 1 bp and up to 4× leverage. Day trading stocks with margin needs $25k in a US account (pattern-day-trader rule).
  • Backtest figures are the 2022+ window where available and were chosen after a large search, so expect live results to come in lower.
  • A Sharpe-1 strategy loses money in about 46% of 4-day windows and 31% of 3-month windows. Short-run results carry almost no information.
